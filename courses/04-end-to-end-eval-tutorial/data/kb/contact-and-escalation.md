# Contacting a Human Agent

## When the assistant hands over to a human
The support assistant passes the conversation to a human agent for: refunds above $500, refunds
outside the return window, warranty claims, injuries or safety incidents involving a product,
legal complaints, and any customer who asks to speak to a person.

## What happens after a handover
The assistant creates a support ticket with a short summary, so the customer does not have to
repeat themselves. A human agent replies by email within 1 business day; safety incidents are
answered within 4 hours.

## Support hours
Human agents work Monday to Friday, 8 am to 8 pm, and Saturday, 9 am to 5 pm (US Eastern time).
The assistant is available at any time.
