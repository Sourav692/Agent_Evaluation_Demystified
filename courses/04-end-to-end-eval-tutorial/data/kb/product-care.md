# Product Care

## Tents
Dry a tent completely before storing it, to prevent mildew. Clean it with cold water and a soft
sponge; never machine-wash a tent or use detergent, which strips the waterproof coating.

## Waterproof jackets
Wash waterproof jackets with a technical cleaner on a cool cycle, then tumble dry on low heat
for 20 minutes to restore the water-repellent finish. Re-proof the jacket once a season.

## Sleeping bags
Store sleeping bags loose in a large cotton sack, never compressed in the stuff sack. Down bags
can be machine-washed on a gentle cycle with down wash, then tumble-dried on low with two clean
tennis balls to break up clumps.
