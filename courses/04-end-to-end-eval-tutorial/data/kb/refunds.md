# Refunds

## When refunds are issued
A refund is issued once the returned item reaches our warehouse and passes inspection, usually
within 3 business days of arrival. Orders that were never shipped are refunded as soon as they
are cancelled.

## How long a refund takes
Refunds go back to the original payment method. Card refunds appear within 5 to 7 business days
after we issue them; PayPal refunds appear within 2 business days. Store credit is available
immediately.

## Shipping costs
Original shipping charges are not refunded, unless the item arrived damaged, defective, or was
the wrong item. Return shipping is free with the prepaid label.

## Refunds the assistant can issue
The support assistant can issue a refund only after confirming the order ID and the email
address on the order. It can refund up to $500 per order. Refunds above $500, and refunds
outside the return window, must be handled by a human support agent.
