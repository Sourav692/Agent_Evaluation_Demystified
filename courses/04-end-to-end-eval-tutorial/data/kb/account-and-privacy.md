# Account and Privacy

## Verifying your identity
Before discussing or changing an order, support confirms the order ID and the email address on
the order. We never share order details, addresses or payment information with anyone other
than the account holder, even if they say they are a family member.

## Password reset
Reset your password from the sign-in page with "Forgot password". The reset link expires after
30 minutes. Support staff cannot see or set passwords.

## Deleting your data
You can ask us to delete your account and personal data. Deletion is completed within 30 days.
Order records needed for tax purposes are kept for 7 years, as the law requires.
