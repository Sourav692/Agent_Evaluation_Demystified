# Warranty

## Standard warranty
Tents, backpacks and sleeping bags carry a 1-year warranty against manufacturing defects,
counted from the delivery date. The warranty covers broken zips, seam failures and faulty
buckles. It does not cover wear and tear, accidents, or damage from misuse.

## Lifetime warranty
Pinecrest-brand trekking poles carry a lifetime warranty. If a pole section breaks during normal
use, we replace that section free of charge, for as long as the original buyer owns the poles.

## Making a warranty claim
To make a claim, contact support with the order ID and photos that show the defect. We reply
within 2 business days with a repair, a replacement, or store credit. Warranty claims are
handled by a human support agent.
