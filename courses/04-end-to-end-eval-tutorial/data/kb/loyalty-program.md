# Trailpoints Loyalty Program

## Earning points
Members earn 1 Trailpoint for every $1 spent. Sale and clearance items earn half points.
Gift card purchases do not earn points.

## Redeeming points
Every 500 Trailpoints can be redeemed for $10 off a future order. Points can be combined with
sale prices but not with other discount codes.

## Points expiry
Trailpoints expire 12 months after they are earned. Returning an item removes the points that
were earned on it.
