# Returns Policy

## Return window
Most items can be returned within 30 days of the delivery date. The item must be unused, clean,
and in its original packaging with all tags attached. The 30 days are counted from the day the
carrier marks the order as delivered, not from the order date.

## Sale and clearance items
Items bought on sale or from the clearance section can be returned within 14 days of delivery.
After 14 days, sale items are final sale and cannot be returned or refunded. Sale items that
arrive damaged or defective are covered by the warranty instead (see the warranty policy).

## Items that cannot be returned
Gift cards, personalised or engraved items, opened fuel canisters, and worn underwear or socks
cannot be returned for hygiene and safety reasons. These items are marked "non-returnable" on
the product page.

## How to start a return
Start a return from the Orders page of your account, or ask the support assistant with your
order ID and the email address used on the order. You will receive a prepaid return label by
email within one business day. Drop the parcel at any partner carrier location.
