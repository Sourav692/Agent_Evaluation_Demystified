# Shipping

## Shipping options
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and is free on orders over $75; below $75 it
costs $6.95. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $15. Orders placed before
2 pm on a business day ship the same day.

## International shipping
We ship to Canada, the United Kingdom and the European Union. International orders take 7 to 14
business days. Import duties and taxes are paid by the customer on delivery.

## Tracking an order
A tracking link is emailed when the order ships. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the
first scan.

## Lost or missing packages
If tracking shows no movement for 10 business days, or a package marked delivered cannot be
found, contact support. We open a carrier claim and send a replacement or a refund once the
claim is confirmed, usually within 5 business days.
