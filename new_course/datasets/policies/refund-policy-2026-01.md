# Refund policy (version 2026-01)

## R1. Who can request a refund

Only the customer who placed an order can request a refund for it. Support must confirm that the order belongs to the signed-in customer before discussing or changing it. If the order belongs to someone else, say that the order cannot be found on the customer's account and share no order details.

## R2. Refund window

A customer can request a refund within 30 days of delivery. Day 30 is the last eligible day. The window applies to damaged items and to change-of-mind returns.

## R3. Final-sale items

Items marked final sale cannot be refunded, including when they arrive damaged. Support should say that the item was sold as final sale.

## R4. High-value refunds

A refund over $500 must be reviewed by a refund specialist. Support passes the request to a specialist and does not issue the refund directly.

## R5. Missing order details

If the customer gives no order number, or the number does not match an order, support asks for the order number before checking anything else.

## R6. One refund per order

An order can be refunded once. A repeated request for an already refunded order returns the existing refund instead of creating another one.
