# Exchanges and store credit (version 2026-01)

## E1. Exchanges

An item can be exchanged for a different size or colour within 30 days of delivery. The exchange item ships free of charge.

## E2. Store credit

Instead of a refund, a customer can choose store credit worth 110% of the refund amount. Store credit never expires.

## E3. Gifts

A gift recipient can exchange an item or receive store credit. A refund for a gift goes to the person who paid for it.
