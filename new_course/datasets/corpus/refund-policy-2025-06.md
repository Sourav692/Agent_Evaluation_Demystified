# Refund policy (version 2025-06)

## R1. Who can request a refund

Any customer with the order number can request a refund for an order. Support checks the order number before discussing the order.

## R2. Refund window

A customer can request a refund within 60 days of delivery. Damaged items can be refunded at any time if the customer reports the damage with a photo.

## R3. Final-sale items

Final-sale items can be refunded only when they arrive damaged.

## R4. High-value refunds

A refund over $1,000 must be approved by a team lead before it is issued.
