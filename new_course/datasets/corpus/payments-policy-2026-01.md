# Payments and refund timing (version 2026-01)

## P1. Where a refund goes

A refund goes back to the original payment method. Support cannot send a refund to a different card or account.

## P2. How long a refund takes

Card refunds appear within 3 to 5 business days after they are issued. Bank transfer refunds can take up to 10 business days.

## P3. Price adjustments

If an item's price drops within 7 days of purchase, the customer can ask for the difference back once per order.
