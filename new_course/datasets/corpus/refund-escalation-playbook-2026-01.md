# Refund escalation playbook (internal, version 2026-01)

## I1. Specialist approval limits

Refund specialists can approve refunds up to $5,000. Refunds above $5,000 need a finance manager.

## I2. Fraud signals

Flag the account for review when a customer requests more than three refunds in 30 days, or asks for a refund to a new card.

## I3. Goodwill credits

Agents can offer a goodwill credit of up to $25 without approval when a delivery is late. Do not mention goodwill credits unless the customer has a late delivery.
