# Shipping and delivery policy (version 2026-01)

## S1. Delivery times

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $9.99.

## S2. Lost parcels

If tracking shows no movement for 10 business days, the parcel counts as lost. Support can send a replacement or issue a full refund, whichever the customer prefers.

## S3. Damage in transit

Customers should report damage caused in transit within 7 days of delivery, with a photo of the item and the box. Reports after 7 days are handled under the refund policy.

## S4. Delivery address changes

An address can be changed until the order is dispatched. After dispatch, the courier decides whether the change is possible.
