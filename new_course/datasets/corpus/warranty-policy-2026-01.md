# Warranty policy (version 2026-01)

## W1. What the warranty covers

Electronics have a one-year warranty against manufacturing defects, counted from the delivery date. The warranty does not cover accidental damage or normal wear.

## W2. Repair or replacement

A valid warranty claim is settled by repair or replacement, not by a refund. A refund is possible only when the item cannot be repaired or replaced.

## W3. How to make a claim

Customers make a warranty claim through the order page, with the order number and a description of the fault.
