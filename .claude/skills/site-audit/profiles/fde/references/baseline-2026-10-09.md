## Verdict at baseline

Content strong, structure not production ready. Three content libraries (a T0–T9 curriculum under `/learn`, numbered modules under `/modules/NN-…`, static reviews under `/fde-last-day-prep/…html`) were merged and each kept its own hub, names and numbering.

- Scores: content 6.0 (3rd of 7), structure 4.4 (last), overall 5.2 (last).
- Core strength: 33 worked AI designs at up to four depths — deeper than any competitor checked. Positioning: "the AI system design and decomposition casebook for FDE interviews".
- Biggest problems: duplicated hubs; five start pages; 39 Blocked badges on first visit; empty Company Prep in the top nav; build artefacts on pages; no About page, share image, canonical tags or analytics.

## What to keep (do not regress)

- Lesson template: breadcrumb, sidebar, contents, prev/next, "Lesson n of m", objectives, takeaways, Check yourself, Mark read.
- Write-first practice loop with hidden model answer, rubric, timer and self-score (CT-09).
- Homepage mini-exercise (FV-06).
- Honest status labels — keep the honesty, change where they appear.
- Custom 404 (PR-07), search, theme toggle, mobile menu and bottom nav, glossary, credits.
- The 33-design casebook (CT-08) and final-week recall material (CT-10).

## Proposed structure the owner is working towards

Top nav cut from 8 items to 5 following the candidate journey, one home and one URL pattern per content set, old URLs 301-redirected:

| Section | URL | Holds | Replaces |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Start | `/start`, `/start/[path]` | Diagnostic, 4 paths, "What the FDE loop tests" | `/learning-map`, `/guide`, `/library`, T0.1 lessons |
| Learn | `/learn/[track]/[module]/[lesson]` | Curriculum tracks named without codes | unchanged |
| Casebook | `/casebook/[slug]` | 33 designs; tabs Interview answer, Deep dive, Quick review, Practise this | `/design`, `/modules/15-fde-case-studies/*`, T5.5 |
| Practice | `/practice/[slug]`, `/practice/behavioural/[slug]`, `/practice/mocks/[slug]` | Worksheets, behavioural + Story Bank, mocks | `/modules/01-…`, `/modules/14-…`, `/interview/*` |
| Final Week | `/final-week/[slug]` | 18 reviews, recall cards, rapid revision, cheat sheet | `/fde-last-day-prep/*`, `/interview/final-week` |
| Footer | `/about`, `/credits`, `/changelog` | About, How it works, Glossary, Credits, Changelog, Feedback | `/library/credits` |

Company Prep hidden until at least 3 packs exist. When the restructure ships, run the crawler with `--legacy <profile>/data/legacy-urls-2026-10-09.txt` (check IA-11). If the owner chose different URLs, map them before judging IA-11.

## Method used at baseline

- Sitemap inventory (~300 URLs), about 50 pages read in full across three parallel reading tracks, browser checks in Chrome, six competitors benchmarked.
- Two first-pass findings were wrong and dropped after browser checks: the 404 page is custom, and diagrams do render after load.
- Not checked: real-phone layout (site blocks framing; window resize unavailable), Lighthouse and accessibility scores, most security headers, paid competitor lessons.
